What the service-node columns mean
Advertised endpoint
Signed hostname or IP address and port from the endpoint descriptor validated by Core. This is an advertisement, not a reachability test.
Service period
Descriptor sequence, active epochs, and the exclusive expiry epoch. A registration expiring at the start of Epoch 5 is active through Epoch 4.
Qualification
Core-reported qualification status for the observed epoch. Pending means qualification has not been finalized.
Reward eligibility
Eligibility for service rewards in the observed payout epoch, based on finalized qualification from the prior source epoch.
Technical identity
EPoSe protocol and descriptor versions, the persistent node ID, and the epoch-specific service verification key.
